About Takealot

Takealot is one of South Africa’s largest online retailers, selling everything from electronics to groceries and delivering to every province. The technology, operations and customer teams are based in Cape Town, with distribution centres in Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal and the Western Cape.

We hire graduates and experienced people across engineering, data, logistics and customer service. New joiners get a mentor, a learning budget and clear growth paths.
