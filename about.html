About CHOSEN

We help South Africans find work that fits their lives.

Started in Sandton in 2019, now in every province.

CHOSEN began as a small recruitment desk placing nurses and accountants in Gauteng. We kept hearing the same thing from candidates: job ads didn’t show salaries, applications disappeared, and nobody called back.

So we built a job site that fixes those three things. Every employer is verified, every listing shows pay, and every application has a status you can follow.
